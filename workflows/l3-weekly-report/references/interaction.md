# L3 weekly intake and validation v1.6

## First intake
请确认本周日期范围。若本地学生日报目录可读取，我会优先读取该周的 `daily-data.json`；只有某天JSON缺失时才读取该日L3日报HTML作为兼容回退。

若某次Math/English专项辅导未写入日报，请补充对应日期、30分钟课次、主要内容和教师反馈。我会校验Math 2/2、English 2/2。

## Blocking gaps
- Requested week is unknown.
- Student/date mismatch.
- A tutoring session count is short and it is unclear whether the record is missing or the session was not delivered.

Do not ask the supervisor to manually total statistics already available in daily data.
